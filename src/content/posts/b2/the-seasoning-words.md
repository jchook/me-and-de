---
title: The Seasoning Words
date: 2026-08-02
level: B2
description: Modalpartikeln — words that mean nothing and change everything.
---

*Modalpartikeln* are small German words that do not have a useful one-word translation. They do not change the basic information in a sentence. They change how the sentence lands.

Start with a simple invitation:

*Komm vorbei.* — Come over.

Then add particles:

- *Komm doch mal vorbei.* — Come over sometime. This feels warm and low-pressure: we are friends, and it would be nice.
- *Komm halt vorbei.* — Come over. There is resignation in it. You were going to come anyway, and the speaker has accepted that.
- *Komm schon vorbei.* — Come over. This is impatient: we both know you want to, so stop acting reluctant.

The instruction is still the same. The relationship between the speakers is not.

The same difference appears in statements. *Das ist ja interessant* expresses surprise. *Das ist schon interessant* is a concession. It means something is interesting, but there is already a *but* behind it.

Particles can also stack:

*Komm doch halt mal eben kurz vorbei*

This is a real sentence. Each particle adjusts the tone slightly, and there is no dictionary entry that can fully explain the result.

Man kann sie nicht lernen. Man kann sie nur irgendwann fühlen. Ich verstehe sie schon — ich verstehe sie halt noch nicht.
