---
title: The Girl Is Neuter
date: 2026-07-05
level: A1
description: On German grammatical gender, and the specific tragedy of das Mädchen.
---

German nouns have three genders, and they were assigned by lottery.

*Der Löffel. Die Gabel. Das Messer.* The spoon is a man, the fork is a woman, and the knife declines to comment. You look for a system. There is no system. The sun is feminine and the moon is masculine, which is the exact opposite of French, presumably out of spite.

Then, in week two, the textbook introduces *das Mädchen*. The girl. Neuter.

There is a reason, and the reason makes it worse: anything ending in *-chen* is a diminutive, and all diminutives are neuter, and the rule outranks the girl. Grammar follows through on this completely. *Das Mädchen liest. Es liest.* The girl reads. *It* reads.

Mark Twain filed the definitive complaint in 1880:

> In German, a young lady has no sex, while a turnip has. Think what overwrought reverence that shows for the turnip, and what callous disrespect for the girl.

My textbook's advice is to simply memorize the gender with every noun, the way one memorizes anything: badly, and with resentment.

*Der Tisch ist ein Mann. Die Tür ist eine Frau. Ich bin müde.*
